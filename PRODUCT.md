# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Product Purpose
Numerical Battle Lab is a read-only collection of 96 numerical cards across 12 rarities. Select two cards, adjust levels, simulate a seeded 1v1 battle, and replay its events.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Preserve the current Level, rarity multipliers, BP, RNG, balance and seed results. Presentation may be rebuilt. Keep Chinese UI, existing brand mark, searchable selection, all statistics, seed replay, three playback speeds and battle summaries.
## Product Principles
Cards are the hero assets. READY must stand on its own. Combat is a continuous product state. Low rarities share the same craftsmanship as collectors.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
Desktop, mobile and 4K; keyboard operation, readable information, reduced motion. No visual effect may conceal combat data.
